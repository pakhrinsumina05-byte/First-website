About Buffalo

Buffalo is a large domestic animal. It is commonly found in Nepal and other Asian countries. Buffaloes are especially popular for their milk production.

Types of Buffalo

1. Murrah Buffalo

2. Nili-Ravi Buffalo

3. Jaffarabadi Buffalo

4. Indigenous Buffalo

Benefits of Buffalo

1. Provides nutritious milk.

2. Helps in agricultural activities.

3. Provides manure for farming.

4. Supports farmers' livelihoods.
